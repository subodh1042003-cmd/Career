OUR STORY

Turning career uncertainty into direction.

Career decisions can be confusing. CareerDecode was created to make the process easier by combining resume analysis, Machine Learning and career intelligence.

Why CareerDecode?

We believe that every resume contains valuable information about a person's potential. Our goal is to decode that information and turn it into meaningful career insights.

01

The Problem

Students and job seekers often struggle to understand which career domain best matches their education, skills and experience. Traditional career guidance can be generic and difficult to personalize.

02

The Idea

CareerDecode was designed around a simple idea: use Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to analyze resume data and provide personalized career insights.

03

The Vision

Our vision is to create a smarter and more accessible career discovery experience where people can understand their strengths, identify skill gaps and confidently explore their future career paths.
