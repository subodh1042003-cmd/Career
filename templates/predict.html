INTELLIGENT ANALYSIS

What Happens After You Click Analyze?

CareerDecode processes your resume through multiple AI and Machine Learning stages before generating your final career result.

01

Resume Upload

Your PDF or DOCX resume is securely received by the Django application.

02

Text Extraction

Useful resume information and professional skills are extracted from the document.

03

NLP + TF-IDF

Resume text is processed and converted into numerical features.

04

ML Prediction

The trained classification model predicts the suitable career domain.

05

Skill Extraction

Technical and professional skills are identified from the resume.

06

Skill Gap

Existing skills are compared with the skills required for the predicted career.

07

Career Recommendation

Career and skill recommendations are prepared from the analysis.

08

Final Result

Your career prediction, score, skills and recommendations are shown on the result page.
